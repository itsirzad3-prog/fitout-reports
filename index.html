Backup / restore

Your reports sync automatically between devices when you sign in. You can still download a full backup (reports + photos) as a safety copy, or restore an older backup, including one from the old local version of this app.

Restore adds the records to your cloud account. If a report ID already exists, the restored record replaces it. Large backups with many photos can take a few minutes.
